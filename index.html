About me

JC

I launched and manage youngs.co.nz, the online store for Youngs Automotive in Nelson.

I designed the site's layout and manage everything from product listings and SEO to Google Ads and Analytics.

I hold a Meta Front-End Developer Professional Certificate and a Certificate in Information Technology and Client Support from Yoobee College, and I enjoy building websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React.

Alongside the website, I work in automotive parts sales at Youngs. Before that, I worked as an operator for companies including Fulton Hogan, Murchison Lime Works and Newcrest Mining, and as a restaurant manager. Those roles built a strong work ethic, attention to detail and the ability to work well with customers and teams.

Featured work

Live site · Website World CMS

Youngs Automotive online store

I launched and manage the website for Youngs Automotive, a Nelson automotive, marine and 4WD parts retailer. The store runs on Website World CMS, and I designed its layout. The site has a full product catalogue, search, monthly specials, a shopping cart and checkout, customer accounts, wishlists and trade account applications.

  • 2,500+ products added. I take the product information supplied by our suppliers and use AI (Claude) to format those details, such as descriptions and specifications, into the site's product page layout before publishing them.
  • SEO. I handle SEO for every product and for the site as a whole, following SEO best practices so customers can find our parts in search results.
  • Google Ads, Merchant Center & Analytics. I implemented these for the site, to advertise our products, list them on Google Shopping and track how visitors use the store.
